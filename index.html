How to advertise diamonds

Key points from the FTC Jewelry Guides

Use precise terminology

Describe laboratory-grown diamonds with clear qualifying terms and avoid language that could imply a mined origin.

Keep every channel consistent

Match your disclosures across web copy, social media, signage, and associate talking points so customers hear the same message.

Back claims with supplier assurances

Use written agreements and testing expectations to confirm that incoming goods are represented accurately before they enter your inventory.

Frequently asked questions

Common disclosure questions

What are laboratory-grown diamonds?

They share the same optical, physical, and chemical properties as mined diamonds, but they are created in a laboratory rather than formed naturally below the earth's surface.

What do customers need to be told?

Customers should receive clear, accurate disclosures whenever a diamond is laboratory-grown, with language that avoids any suggestion that the stone is natural unless it truly is.

How should the supplier agreements be used?

They work best as negotiation tools for new vendors and as customizable documentation for existing suppliers so your team can secure clear assurances about how goods are identified and described.
